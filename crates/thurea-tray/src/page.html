VERSE OF THE DAY
SET UP A PHONE WITH THUREA

  1. Start with a new phone, or factory-reset it (this erases it).
  2. On the first Welcome screen, tap the same spot 6 times. A scanner opens.
  3. Scan this code. The phone installs Thurea from this computer, and Thurea is in charge before the home screen appears.
  1. On the phone: Settings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times. (Or plug it in by USB with USB debugging on, and skip to step 3.)
  2. Settings → System → Developer options → Wireless debugging: on → Pair device with QR code.
  3. Scan this code. Thurea installs over Wi-Fi, signs your accounts out for a moment (Android needs that once), takes charge, then signs you back in. Nothing is erased; you enter passwords on the phone.
